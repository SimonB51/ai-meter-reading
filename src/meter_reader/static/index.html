How it works

Four models,
one reading.

Step 1 of 4Find the meter

A YOLO detector finds the counter window anywhere in the photo, at any distance.

Step 2 of 4Straighten it

Rotated boxes locate the black and red digit drums. Their positions give the exact angle.

Step 3 of 4Read it four ways

An OCR model reads the digit band from four slightly different views and checks that they agree.

Step 4 of 4Know when to ask

Confident: accepted. Unsure: a human checks, and the correction becomes training data.
