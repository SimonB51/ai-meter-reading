123Meter Reader

Read a water meter
from a photo.

Upload any photo of a meter: straight, tilted or upside down. You get the reading in about a second, with a confidence score. When the model isn't sure, it says so and sends the photo to a human.

No meter nearby? Tap one of these:
01Find the meterA YOLO model locates the counter in the photo.
02Straighten itRotated boxes find the black and red digits and give the angle.
03Read it twiceAn OCR model and a CNN trained on meters read the digits independently.
04Know when to askWhen the two agree, the reading is accepted. When they don't, a human checks it.
